Did you know?

Small things, big differences

🍽️
Free lunch

Since 2023, every primary school pupil gets a free school meal.

📵
No phones in lessons

In primary schools phones stay in the bag, and a ban during breaks is planned.

🔄
5 or 1?

In Croatia 5 is the best grade. In Germany 1 is the best, and a 5 means you failed!
